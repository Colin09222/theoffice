What I fix

The everyday fixes that fit a pop-up stand:

  • Flat tyres and tubes: patched or swapped so you can ride home.
  • Brakes: adjusted so they stop smoothly and don't rub.
  • Gears: tuned so they shift cleanly and stop skipping.
  • Chain: cleaned and lubed so it runs quietly.

Not sure if your problem is on the list? Bring it along and ask. If it's a bigger job than a stand can handle, I'll tell you straight.
