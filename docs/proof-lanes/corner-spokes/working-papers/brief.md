# Corner Spokes — Site Brief

## What the site is for
Corner Spokes is Maya's Saturday bike repair stand in her neighbourhood. The site tells local riders what she fixes and lets them join a list so Maya can tell them when and where she'll be out next.

## Who it is for
Local riders in Maya's neighbourhood who need a small fix: a flat, rubbing brakes, skipping gears, a noisy chain.

## The one thing a visitor should do
**Join the list** (email, optional first name). Everything on the page leads to the sign-up form.

## What we know
- Runs on Saturdays only.
- It's a pop-up stand, run by Maya.

## What is NOT settled (do not invent or hint at)
- Location / address
- Prices (no "free", no ranges)
- Exact hours
- The fix list itself — Maya must confirm it before launch.

## Rules for the page
- No testimonials, customer counts, experience claims or certifications.
- No email address or phone number on the page.
- Form uses Office.submitForm('waitlist', …) with a hidden "website" spam-trap field.
- Nobody here can send emails. Maya (or the boss) reads the sign-ups on the project card and contacts people herself. Nothing goes out automatically.

## Voice
Friendly, plain, neighbourly. Short sentences. First person from Maya.
