Three good used paperbacks a month, picked around one theme.

Second Shelf is a planned monthly box of second-hand paperback novels for US readers who get through a lot of books and would rather pay used-book prices than new.

How it works

  1. See the themeOne theme a month, shared ahead of time.
  2. We pick 3 novelsUsed paperbacks across genres: maybe a mystery, a literary novel and a romance.
  3. Open your boxThree books plus a card on why each one made the cut.
