# Second Shelf: short business plan

*Draft plan, November 2026. **Second Shelf has not launched.** There are no customers, no suppliers, no sales and no waitlist sign-ups yet. Every price here is **proposed** until Priya sets it. Every worked-out number is an **Estimate**, with its assumption. Facts from the web link to their source. Built on Arlo's research and Otis's numbers.*

---

## Target customer

US adults who read a lot and buy on a budget: people who get through a book or more a month and would rather pay used-book prices than new.

- The average US adult read **12.6 books** in 2021, and **27%** read more than 10. Women (15.7 books on average) and college graduates (14.6) read the most. ([Gallup, 2022](https://news.gallup.com/poll/388541/americans-reading-fewer-books-past.aspx))
- A warning sign: the heaviest-reading groups are reading less. The share of women reading more than 10 books a year fell from 41% to 32%. (same source)
- **Estimate:** about 70 million US heavy readers. *Assumption: 27% of roughly 260 million US adults. The adult population figure is assumed, not sourced.* This is a ceiling, not a forecast.
- We don't yet have sourced figures for what these readers spend, or for what annoys them about current boxes. The one complaint we found was about a different box: "Pricy handling fee AND shipping fee." ([Cratejoy](https://www.cratejoy.com/blogs/box-insider/cheap-book-subscription-boxes))

## The offer and pricing

**What's in a box:** 3 second-hand paperback novels, all chosen to fit that month's theme, plus a printed theme card that says why each book made the cut. Books only, with no merch, so the box stays eligible for USPS Media Mail ([DMM 173](https://pe.usps.com/text/dmm300/173.htm)).

**How themes work:** each month has one theme, announced ahead of time. Themes cut across genres: a theme might bring together a mystery, a literary novel and a romance. The rivals we checked sort by genre or send a "blind date" book. None of the boxes we found is built around a monthly adult theme, though we only checked a few listings.

**Example themes (ideas only):**
- *Islands and Lighthouses*: stories set on the edge of the sea
- *Letters and Diaries*: novels told in letters or journals
- *Small Towns, Big Secrets*
- *Strangers on a Train*: journeys that go wrong
- *Book Club Favorites from 20 Years Ago*

**Pricing (all proposed):**

| Plan | Price | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly, 3 books, US shipping included | **$17.99 (proposed)** | Recommended starting point. About $6 a book. |
| Budget option | $14.99 (proposed) | Only works if books cost close to $1.50 each (see The numbers). |
| Premium option | $19.99 (proposed) | More margin, but further above the main rival's price. |

At $17.99 we cost more per book than Used Books Monthly. The theme and the curation have to earn that difference.

**Approach:** start by buying mixed lots and hand-picking from local sales. Then sort the stock into theme piles and plan themes around what's actually on hand, not the other way round. Random lots rarely match a theme, which pushes costs up.

## The numbers

All figures are for one 3-paperback box. Postage rates are from [Postalguidance](https://postalguidance.com/media-mail-cost/) (Media Mail: 1 lb $4.39, 2 lb $5.13, 3 lb $5.86, effective July 12, 2026; not yet checked against [USPS Notice 123](https://pe.usps.com/text/dmm300/Notice123.htm)).

| | Low | Mid | High |
|---|---|---|---|
| Box cost before payment fee | $9.84 | $14.13 | $20.91 |
| Margin at $17.99 (proposed) | $7.33 | $3.04 | –$3.74 |
| Margin at $19.99 (proposed) | $9.27 | $4.98 | –$1.80 |
| Margin at $14.99 (proposed) | $4.42 | $0.13 | –$6.65 |

- **Box cost: Estimate.** *Assumptions: books at $1.50 / $2.50 / $4.00 each (based on the eBay lot prices); mailer and theme card at $0.50 / $0.75 / $1.25 (not checked); postage in the 1 / 2 / 3 lb band; a 10% / 10% / 15% allowance for damaged or unusable books.*
- **Margins: Estimate.** *Assumption: price minus box cost minus a payment fee of 2.9% + $0.30. That fee is a typical card rate, not confirmed for Priya's processor. Labor is not included.*
- **Break-even at $17.99: Estimate,** 14 subscribers (low cost) or 33 (mid cost). At high cost it never breaks even. *Assumption: $100 a month in fixed costs (platform, domain, supplies). This is a placeholder, not a quote.* These are not forecasts of sign-ups.
- **Time: Estimate,** 20–40 minutes per box, or about 7–13 hours a month at 20 boxes. *Assumption: books are hand-picked from mixed lots or local sales.* At an assumed $15/hr that adds $5–$10 a box, so at first the owner's time is effectively unpaid.
- **What makes or breaks it:** (1) **book cost.** Every $1 per book adds $3 per box. Under about $2.50 a book the box works at $17.99. At $4 a book it loses money at every proposed price. (2) **Weight.** Mass-market paperbacks keep the box under 1 lb. Trade paperbacks push it to 2–3 lb.

## Only you can do these

**Steps only Priya can do.** Nobody in this office can buy, sign up, post or contact anyone, and none of this happens by itself:

- **Find and buy from suppliers:** order bulk lots on eBay or Etsy, and go to local thrift stores and library sales.
- **Set up a Stripe Payment Link** and paste it on the project so the site can link to it.
- **Buy mailers, labels and postage,** and a postal scale.
- **Form the business** and sort out **sales tax** (registration and whether subscriptions are taxable where you sell). Check with an accountant or your state if you're unsure.
- **Announce the waitlist:** post about it wherever your readers are.
- **Email the people who sign up.** We can draft the messages, but only you can send them.
- **Talk to real readers** about themes, price and what they'd pay, before you commit to stock.
- **Handle customer service, returns and refunds** once boxes ship.
