All margins are **Estimates**: price minus box cost minus payment fee. They leave out labor.

**Competitor prices, from the research:**
- Used Books Monthly: 2 books for $10.99 and 4 books for $18.99 (https://www.usedbooksmonthly.com/subscribe/). That is about $4.75–$6 per book (**Estimate**), and the page doesn't say whether shipping is included.
- Lightly Used Books Blind Date: $15.00 plus shipping (https://www.lightlyusedbooks.com/products/blind-date-monthly-subscription).
- History Book Box: 3 books from $21.39 (https://www.cratejoy.com/blogs/box-insider/cheap-book-subscription-boxes).

At $17.99 or $19.99, Second Shelf costs more per book than Used Books Monthly. The monthly theme has to justify the difference.

## 3. Break-even and time

**Fixed cost assumption, Estimate**: $100 a month for a store or subscription platform, a domain, label and printing supplies, and a share of a scale. This is a placeholder, not a quote.

| Proposed price | Subscribers to cover $100/mo, low cost | Mid cost | High cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| $14.99 | 23 | ~770 (not viable) | never |
| $17.99 | 14 | 33 | never |
| $19.99 | 11 | 21 | never |

All counts are **Estimates**: $100 divided by the margin per box, rounded up. They are not a forecast of how many people will subscribe.

**Hours, Estimate**: 15–30 minutes of sourcing and theme-matching per box, plus 5–10 minutes of packing and labelling. That makes about **20–40 minutes per box**, or about 7–13 hours a month at 20 boxes. Assumption: books are hand-picked from mixed lots or thrift stores. If that time were paid at $15/hr (assumed rate), it would add **$5–$10 per box** (**Estimate**). That wipes out most of the mid-case margin, so at first the owner's time is effectively unpaid.

## 4. The numbers that make or break it

1. **Book cost per copy.** Every $1 per book adds $3 per box. Below about $2.50 a book the box works at $17.99 or more. At $4 a book it loses money at every proposed price. Sourcing themed books cheaply is the whole business.
2. **Box weight (the postage band).** Three mass-market paperbacks fit under 1 lb ($4.39). Trade paperbacks push the box to 2–3 lb ($5.13–$5.86). Weigh real samples before setting a price. Merch would also make the box ineligible for Media Mail (https://pe.usps.com/text/dmm300/173.htm).
