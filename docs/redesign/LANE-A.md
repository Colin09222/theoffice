2. **j02 Quiet the noise** (`office-j02-quiet`). (a) Demo floors 1 and 2 (the filler/demo floors; find how they're marked) disappear from every public list: directory, lift, ticker, Launched page, hero. Keep them reachable for the admin. Switch: an env var to show them again. (b) No model-written banter: the banter writer (see the banter commits on main: `BANTER_*` env, freshLines, quotesHonest) makes zero model calls. Replace it with a fixed list of 200 pre-written lines in a data file, picked at random, shown only as speech bubbles in the 3D room, never in Talk/the timeline. Lines are light office small talk, no names of real people, no boss quotes, no money. (c) No "Closed", "gone home", "Open 9-10" anywhere a visitor sees: a floor shows one of four states, Working, Waiting on boss, Idle ("last active 2h ago"), Paused (no reason shown). Hours stay a boss setting. (d) The public ticker shows real work only (no small talk, no demo floors).

Missing screenshot: the coloured initial card used today. Visits/plays: reuse whatever the Numbers tab counts; bots, the boss and admin views must not count (say in DONE.md what you could and couldn't exclude). The orchestrator runs it on the live server after deploy: look at how screenshots are taken today (ship check / live check / the runner's `office-runner/browser` Playwright image) and make the script runnable there; put the exact command in DEPLOY NOTES. Live has 6 real launches; done means all 6 show a screenshot and 3 show on the hero (the orchestrator checks this live; you prove it with tests and fixtures).
