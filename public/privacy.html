Privacy at The Office

Plain words. Contact: office@iscolinfree.com

If you just walk in and watch

You need no account. A short-lived cookie-free visit count is kept for published projects: a hash of the day, the project, your address and browser, salted so it can't be reversed, and those hashes are thrown away after a day. It counts visitors; it doesn't follow you.

If you run a floor

We store what your company needs to work: its name and settings, your chat with your manager, the tasks, files the staff make, and anything you upload. Your floor's login is a one-time recovery code, and only a hash of it is stored. The session cookie is only for this site and is not readable by page scripts.

Your messages and files, and the work the staff do from them, are sent to the AI model providers that do the work (currently Anthropic and OpenRouter, and whichever models they route to). They don't get anything beyond what the task needs, and nothing here is sold.

Email

If your company has its own mailbox, mail to and from it passes through Resend, and is kept on your floor. Email to anyone new waits for your OK.

Things your company publishes

A game or website your staff publish can keep saves, scores, accounts or form entries for its visitors through Office Cloud, on that project alone. Passwords are stored as hashes, and a project can only read its own data. If you want anything deleted, email us.

Google Ads, if you connect it

The Office uses Google sign-in only if a boss chooses to connect a Google Ads account, and only to create and manage the ads that boss asks for and approves. Access to your Google data is not sold, not shared with anyone else, and not used for anything else. The Office's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. You can disconnect at any time from your Google Account, and ask us to delete what is stored.

What we don't do

  • No third-party trackers or analytics on The Office.
  • No selling of your data.
  • No model training on your data by The Office. The AI providers' own terms apply to what they receive.

Deleting things

Email office@iscolinfree.com from the address you want to talk about, or with your floor's name, and we'll delete your floor, a project, or what we hold about you. There's no fancy process, because this is a small personal project.
