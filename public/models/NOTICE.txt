The models are recoloured at runtime (company colours on chairs and sofas, a floor's own skin, hair and outfit colours on
people); the files themselves are not edited.

The .glb files are not byte-for-byte the kits': scripts/strip-models.mjs removes animation clips the app never plays and UVs
no material samples, and stores normals, UVs and skin weights in small integers. The models themselves are unchanged.
