The models are recoloured at runtime (company colours on chairs and sofas, a floor's own skin, hair and outfit colours on
people); the files themselves are not edited.

The .glb files are not byte-for-byte the kits': scripts/strip-models.mjs removes animation clips the app never plays and UVs
no material samples, and stores normals, UVs and skin weights in small integers. The models themselves are unchanged.

People under public/models/people
----------------------------------
The office's people are built from Quaternius's Ultimate Modular Men and Ultimate Modular Women packs (https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimatemodularcharacters.html and
.../ultimatemodularwomen.html), downloaded on 8 November 2026 from the Google Drive folders those pages link to, "Individual Characters/glTF". The License.txt in each pack:

  Ultimate Modular Males by @Quaternius
  License: CC0 1.0 Universal (CC0 1.0), Public Domain Dedication, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
  Credit is not required; we credit Quaternius anyway.

Characters used: men Casual_2, Casual_Hoodie, Farmer, Suit, Worker, Punk; women Casual, Formal, Worker, Suit, Adventurer, Punk (mapped to male-a..f and female-a..f in that
order). scripts/pack-people.mjs merges each into one skinned mesh on a 22-bone rig (the fingers are folded into the wrist), welds the flat-shaded vertices, drops normals and UVs,
and records each part's role (skin, hair, top, legs and so on) so a person can be recoloured. clips.glb holds the kit's Idle_Neutral, Walk, Interact and Wave clips, resampled.
The packs' other files (FBX, blends) were not copied. The Universal Base Characters and Universal Animation Library packs were looked at and not used.

The floor and wall textures are listed separately, in public/assets/NOTICE.txt.
