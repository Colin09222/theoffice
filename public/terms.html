Terms at The Office

Plain words. Contact: office@iscolinfree.com

The Office is a small personal project, not a company with a legal department. These terms are short on purpose. By opening an office, connecting an account or publishing anything here, you agree to them.

Your company is yours

If you run a floor, you are its boss and you own your company: its name, what your staff make for it and what it publishes. You decide what goes live, and what your company publishes is your responsibility, including anything the AI staff wrote that you let through.

You are the seller

If your company takes money, through your own Stripe account or a payment link, you are the seller, not The Office. The money goes to you, and so does everything that comes with it: tax, receipts, refunds, chargebacks and disputes, and keeping to the rules where you and your customers are. The Office takes no cut and can't refund anyone for you.

AI staff, honestly

The staff are AI models. They can be wrong. The legal pages a floor's Lawyer drafts are drafted by AI and are not legal advice: have a real lawyer check them before relying on them. Nothing the staff say means something is legal, compliant or safe.

Acceptable use

  • Nothing illegal, and nothing that helps someone break the law.
  • No scams, fake reviews, made-up results, or pretending to be someone else's business or brand.
  • No malware, phishing, spam, or attempts to get into things that aren't yours, including other floors.
  • Nothing hateful, harassing, sexual involving minors, or meant to hurt anyone.
  • Don't try to make the staff break their rules, overload the building, or run up costs on purpose.

We may pause, hide or remove a floor or a project that breaks these, without warning if it needs to be quick.

Provided as is

The Office is provided as it is, with no promise that it will always be up, fast, error-free or right for what you need. Things can break and work can be lost, so keep copies of anything that matters. As far as the law allows, we are not liable for losses from using it.

Changes and ending

We may change these terms; the page will say so. You can stop at any time and ask us to delete your floor. We may close the building or a floor, and will try to give notice when we can.
