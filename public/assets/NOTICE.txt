Third-party textures under public/assets/3d
===========================================

All are CC0 1.0 (public domain, http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/), downloaded on 7 November 2026 straight from
the original sites. Credit is not required; we give it anyway. Each set was resized and re-compressed (colour 512 px, normal 384 px,
roughness 256 px, JPEG) and wrapped in a small .glb by scripts/pack-textures.mjs, because the static server only serves a short list of
file types. The pixels are otherwise unchanged. Nothing here comes from any other site.
